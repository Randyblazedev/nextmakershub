01 / About Us

More than learning. It's about becoming.

NextMakers Hub is designed to give students opportunities to explore different areas of technology, develop useful life skills and discover what they are capable of achieving.

We believe learning becomes more meaningful when students can experiment, ask questions, collaborate and apply what they learn to real-world challenges.

04 / Our Approach

Learn by doing. Grow by exploring.

We believe students should have the opportunity to experience learning beyond textbooks. That's why the program places emphasis on practical exploration, experimentation and participation.

Learning activities and digital experiences will depend partly on the materials and resources available. However, available resources will not define the limits of curiosity, creativity or the learning process.

A few things you might want to know about the program.

NextMakers Hub is a learning initiative designed to introduce students to technology, practical project development, communication and other important life skills.

The program introduces students to different learning areas. Specific entry requirements and experience expectations should be confirmed with the program organizers.
